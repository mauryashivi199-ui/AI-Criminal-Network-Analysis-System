## Slide 2: The Problem
- **Massive Data Silos:** Police officers analyze 10,000+ pages of CDRs, bank records, and FIRs manually.
- **Hidden Kingpins:** Cartel leaders rarely talk directly to victims; they operate through 3-4 layers of burner phones and mule accounts.
- **Slow Linkage:** Manual cross-case correlation takes weeks, allowing suspects to flee abroad or destroy evidence.

---

## Slide 3: Our Solution - KavachNet-AI
- **Multi-Source AI Ingestion:** Automatically ingests FIRs, CDR dumps, and Bank CSVs.
- **Criminal Knowledge Graph:** Reconstructs multi-relational graphs of Suspects, Gangs, Phones, Bank Accounts, and Vehicles.
- **Algorithmic Kingpin Detection:** Identifies syndicate masterminds using PageRank & Betweenness Centrality.
- **AI Link Prediction:** Uncovers hidden / unobserved relationships before crimes escalate.

---

## Slide 4: Key Modules & Architecture
1. **Interactive Force-Directed Network Graph:** Real-time visual manipulation, degree of separation tracing, and sub-gang clustering.
2. **CDR & Burner Phone Radar:** Detects nocturnal spikes (1 AM – 5 AM) and multiple IMEI handset swaps.
3. **Financial Smurfing & Crypto Tracker:** Spots rapid mule account pass-throughs and TRC-20 USDT offramps.
4. **Geo-Spatial Crime Map:** Spatial hotspot mapping of safe houses and transit corridors.
5. **AI Forensic Copilot:** Natural language Graph-RAG assistant answering tactical queries.
6. **Court Case Dossier Generator:** Instant judicial-ready charge sheet evidence summaries.
